Raid Waiting Arcade

Addon · games for the break

  • Twenty simple games — relax or kill time on a break
  • Four support two players: Chess, Checkers, Pairs, Chests
  • Stays out of the pull: combat starts, the game pauses and leaves the screen
  • Chat still works with the window open, and a game survives /reload
  • Installs on its own, nothing else to install

Use: fill the break without leaving the game.

Requires: World of Warcraft 3.3.5a or Retail (Midnight)
