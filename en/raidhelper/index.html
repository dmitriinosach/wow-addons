WoW 3.3.5a addon · version 1.7

What happened in that wipe, and who started it.

A meter tells you how much. Raid Helper tells you what happened and when: who took the mechanic, who stopped getting healed, whose death pulled the raid down after it. The review opens right after the fight, without leaving the game.

Commands

/mrh
Open or close the window. The buttons on the small panel do the same.
/mrh raid
Straight to a tab. Also log — review, spam — recruit spam, gp — mistakes and GP.
/mrh status
Is it recording and how much storage is used. Turn it on and off — /mrh on, /mrh off, /mrh pause.
/mrh help
All commands to chat.

The whole night, attempt by attempt

Recording starts by itself in a raid instance. When the fight ends, the review is ready: click the attempt on the left.

Features A review you don't have to upload

  1. Attempt summary

    Result, raid DPS and HPS, deaths and boss mechanics as compact tables: damage to adds and Val'kyr, stacks, puddles, interrupts. Rules for Icecrown Citadel, the Ruby Sanctum and Trial of the Crusader.

  2. Deaths with causes

    The last hits before each death, which defensives were ready and where the player stood. On a wipe — who it started with.

  3. Timeline and threat

    Casts, auras, incoming damage and the health curve of every player on one strip. Threat is recorded like Omen does it: who held each enemy, aggro pulls and taunts.

  4. 3D replay

    The fight played back on the room floor: players, boss, adds, puddles and cones. Rooms of Icecrown Citadel, the Ruby Sanctum and Trial of the Crusader come from the client's own geometry. Speed from x0.5 to x8.

  5. Raid summary

    The whole night in one window: bosses, wipes, time in combat and idle, consumables per player and their cost in gold, raid awards from Lightning rod to Puddle lover.

  6. Fault Ledger

    Mechanic mistakes turn into GP, EP, DKP or a roll penalty — with proof in chat. EPGP and Quick DKP V2, your own rules and a journal with undo.

  7. Raid gathering

    Recruit spam to channels, raid templates, auto-invite on "+", the raid by groups and its expected DPS. On a ready check you see who lacks buffs, flask or food.

  8. Always at hand

    Mini window: DPS during combat, mistakes after it. A panel with DPS and HPS graphs and the raid's consumables. Lockouts of your characters and the guild on their own tab.

Install How to install

Two ways: the updater installs and updates for you, the archive is by hand.

ManacodeUpdate

  1. Download and run the updater.
  2. Press Install next to Raid Helper.
  3. For 3D rooms — 3D replay rooms → Apply.
Download the updater

By hand

  1. Download an archive: full — with 3D rooms, lite — without them.
  2. Unpack it here: game folder\Interface\AddOns
  3. Fully restart the game and type /mrh.
Releases on GitHub

The Lich King

The 3.3.5 client loses positions on the Lich King platform after Quake. A client patch with instructions comes in the release archive, and in the updater it is one button — "Fix Lich King positions". Only the player who records the fight needs it.
